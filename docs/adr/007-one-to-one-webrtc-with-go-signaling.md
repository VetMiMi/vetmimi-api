# 007 — One-to-one WebRTC with Go WebSocket signaling

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-10-04

## Context

Sessions are online only for now. The booking requirements leave the
"meeting-link process" undecided; the owner wants in-browser video sessions
with authenticated rooms, lifecycle management and reconnect handling. A
session is Daw Mi and one client. Group sessions, when they return, will be
arranged by enquiry.

## Options considered

- **External meeting links (Zoom, Meet).** No code, no privacy guarantees,
  the link process the requirements worried about. Kept as a fallback setting.
- **A hosted video API (Daily, Twilio).** Reliable, costs per minute, another
  account to own.
- **Peer-to-peer WebRTC with our own signaling and a TURN relay.** Browsers do
  the media; the API only exchanges offers, answers and ICE candidates. Right
  size for 1:1. Chosen.

## Decision

- A confirmed online appointment gets a `video_rooms` row when it is confirmed.
  The client's confirmation and reminder emails carry a join link
  `/session/<token>` on the site; Daw Mi joins from the appointment in
  `/admin`. Tokens are random 32-byte, single-appointment, valid from 15
  minutes before `starts_at` until 60 minutes after `ends_at`.
- The site's `/session/<token>` page asks Next, which asks the API, for a
  short-lived (5 minute) **room ticket**; the browser then opens
  `wss://api…/video/rooms/<room>/ws?ticket=…` directly — the one exception to
  ADR-002. The API accepts at most one client and one practitioner connection
  per room; a reconnect with the same ticket subject replaces the stale
  connection.
- Signaling messages are small JSON (`join`, `offer`, `answer`, `ice`,
  `leave`, `peer-state`), relayed to the other participant and never stored.
- Room lifecycle (`waiting`, `in_session`, `ended`) is driven by both peers
  being connected and by either party ending the session or the window
  closing; `ended` marks the appointment `completed` only if Daw Mi confirms
  the prompt in `/admin`.
- ICE: Google STUN plus our own **coturn** on the live host (ADR-005) with
  time-limited credentials generated by the API per ticket. Without TURN, a
  meaningful share of mobile and corporate networks cannot connect.
- The settings row `meeting_link_mode` is `vetmimi_room` or `manual_link`;
  in manual mode Daw Mi pastes a link per appointment and no room is created.
- No recording, ever. The signaling server logs connection events only.

## Consequences

- Media never touches our servers except as an encrypted relay through TURN;
  the privacy story is simple.
- Group sessions are out of scope for this design; they would need an SFU
  (LiveKit) and a new ADR.
- The join page in `vetmimi-next` needs careful states: too early, expired,
  waiting for Daw Mi, connecting, poor connection, ended — each designed, not
  improvised.
